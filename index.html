Hermes

Hermes adalah alat pribadi milik satu orang (Devon). Hermes membaca grup WhatsApp kuliah secara read-only, mencatat tugas dan jadwal, lalu menaruhnya di Google Tasks dan Google Calendar milik pemiliknya sendiri.

Hermes is a single-user personal tool. It records coursework deadlines and events from the owner's study groups into the owner's own Google Tasks and Google Calendar. It is not offered to the public.
